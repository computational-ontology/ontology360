The ring

The norm returns to the circumstance it must answer to — that is the 360. Hover a link to see it; open it to meet the operator that does the work there.

What each neighbour gains

1CircumstanceFor the jurist: the circumstance is what no amendment can reach — natality, plurality, mortality — so a constitution is judged by what it does with an in-emendable ground, not by its text alone.
2RegisterFor the data scientist: a register is not a proxy for belonging; it is belonging as a social object. Coding a constitution is coding an inscribed act, which is why every unit carries its span and its coder.
3Domains · dominationFor the social theorist: domination becomes measurable once it is defined as control of who is inscribed and who controls the inscription — one register per domain, four domains, no fifth.
4Constitutional architectureFor the comparativist: the Comparative Constitutions Project turns the meta-register into a corpus with stable ids, so the same reading can be repeated by someone who disagrees with the author.
5ConsequenceFor everyone: the last link is never computed. What ought to follow for plural democracy is written by a person, signed, and marked as such.
