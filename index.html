The chain

Five links, one circle

Nothing here is asserted from a single discipline. Every important claim runs the whole chain, and every page tells you which link you are standing on.

01 · Being

Circumstance

What resists amendment: natality, plurality, mortality, the Earth. Arendt, Ortega, Onnis.

→
02 · Register

Inscription

Object = inscribed act. The signature individuates; the shared inscription makes a “we”. Ferraris.

Domination

Four domains, one register of belonging each. Domination is control of who is inscribed. Romero.

Meta-register

Where belongings are reconciled or imposed. Read on the Comparative Constitutions Project.

Plural constitutionalism

The consequence — stated by the author, never generated by a technique.
